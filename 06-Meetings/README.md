# 06 – Meetings

## Supervision meetings
After each meeting, copy `_template.md` to `YYYY-MM-DD.md` (the meeting date)
and fill it in within 24 hours.

Semester 1 meeting dates (from the attendance register):
- Week 3 – 2026-10-01
- Week 4 – 2026-10-08
- Week 5 – 2026-10-15
- Week 6 – 2026-10-22
- Weeks 7–10 – <add dates>

**Past meetings:** if you write a note after the event, add this line at the top:
`Reconstructed on YYYY-MM-DD from emails and notes.`

If you missed a meeting, still create the file and write `Absent – informed`
(or similar) with any actions you were given.

## Email supervision
Record important email exchanges in `email-log.md`: date, topic and decision.
Save key threads as PDFs in your OneDrive folder.
